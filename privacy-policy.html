Privacy & Data Care Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you send a finance enquiry through this website, we may collect your basic contact details such as your name, mobile phone number, city or village, and the type of finance you are interested in.

2. What We Never Ask For

We do not ask for sensitive personal information such as bank passwords, ATM PINs, OTP codes, or payment card CVVs on this website. Please do not share passwords or security codes with anyone.

3. How We Use Your Information

We only use the contact details you provide to:

  • Respond to your finance questions and loan enquiries.
  • Explain the service requirements, eligibility, and process.
  • Coordinate appointments with our local team in Telangana.

4. Data Protection

We respect your privacy. We do not sell or rent your personal contact information to third-party telemarketers.

5. Sample Website Notice

This is a sample website created for client review and demonstration. The final policy can be reviewed and updated by Ravi Kanth Finance to reflect applicable business practices and legal guidelines.
